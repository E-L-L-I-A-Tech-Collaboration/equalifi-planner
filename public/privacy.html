Privacy Policy — EqualiF.I. Planner

Last updated: October 3, 2026 · Axiom Zeta Innovations LLC

EqualiF.I. Planner ("the app") is an educational financial-independence assessment tool. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information you provide

The planner works with financial information you enter: household profile, income and cash flow, debts, assets, retirement and education goals, insurance protection inputs, and risk questionnaire answers. This information is used only to calculate your planning results inside the app.

Where your data lives

  • Guest mode: your inputs are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • Signed-in mode: if you create an account, your plans sync to Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) so you can access them across devices. Data is stored under your account and is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Accounts

Sign-in is provided by Firebase Authentication (email/password or Google). We receive your email address and display name for account operation. You can delete your account and your cloud data at any time from inside the app (account menu → Delete Account), or by contacting us at the address below. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — manage subscriptions in Google Play.

Purchases

Subscriptions in the Android app are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google Play shares purchase/subscription status with the app so premium features can be unlocked.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. Standard hosting logs may record IP address, browser type, and timestamps for security and reliability.

Educational tool — not financial advice

EqualiF.I. Planner provides educational estimates based on information and assumptions you supply. It does not provide individualized investment, tax, legal, insurance, or retirement advice, and it is not a bank, broker, or financial advisor.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
